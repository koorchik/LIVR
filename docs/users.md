# Who is using LIVR

Logos are submitted by company and project representatives. These companies may or may not use LIVR on their main web properties, but they definitely use it somewhere in their organizations 🙂

## Add your logo

Are you using LIVR? Please add your logo!

- **[Submit a pull request](https://github.com/koorchik/LIVR/edit/master/docs/data/users.json)**: put the logo into `docs/public/assets/users/` and add it to [`docs/data/users.json`](https://github.com/koorchik/LIVR/blob/master/docs/data/users.json).
- **[Or create an issue](https://github.com/koorchik/LIVR/issues/new)** with a link to the logo and the name of your company or project.
